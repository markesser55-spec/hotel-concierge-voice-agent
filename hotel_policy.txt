The Grand Horizon Resort & Spa - Guest Policy Document

1. General Information

1.1 Check-In and Check-Out Times
- Check-In Time: 3:00 PM local time. Early check-in is subject to availability and may incur an additional fee of $50.
- Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM local time. Late check-out up to 2:00 PM can be arranged for a fee of $75, subject to availability. Check-outs after 2:00 PM will be charged a full night's rate.
- Minimum Age: Guests must be at least 21 years of age to check into a room. A valid government-issued photo ID is required upon arrival.

1.2 Payment and Incidental Holds
- Accepted Payment: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover). Cash is not accepted for the initial deposit but may be used at check-out.
- Incidental Hold: A hold of $100 per night will be placed on your credit card at check-in to cover potential incidental charges. This hold will be released within 3-5 business days after check-out, depending on your financial institution.

2. Amenities and Services

2.1 Room Service (In-Room Dining)
- Hours of Operation: Room service is available daily from 6:00 AM to 11:30 PM.
- Late Night Menu: A limited late-night menu is available from 11:30 PM to 2:00 AM.
- Delivery Fee: A $5.00 delivery fee and an automatic 18% gratuity are added to all in-room dining orders.

2.2 Pool and Fitness Center
- Pool Hours: The outdoor heated pool and hot tub are open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Adult-only swim hours are from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
- Fitness Center Hours: The fitness center is accessible 24 hours a day with a valid guest room key.
- Rules: No glass containers are allowed in the pool area. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult in both the pool and fitness center.

2.3 The Horizon Spa
- Hours of Operation: Tuesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed on Mondays.
- Reservations: Advance reservations are highly recommended. Walk-ins are accepted based on availability.
- Cancellation: Spa appointments must be canceled at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations made within 24 hours will incur a 50% charge of the scheduled service fee.

3. Parking and Transportation

3.1 On-Site Parking
- Valet Parking: Available 24/7 at a rate of $45 per night, which includes unlimited in-and-out privileges.
- Self-Parking: Available in the attached garage at a rate of $25 per night.
- EV Charging: Four Level 2 electric vehicle charging stations are located on the first floor of the self-parking garage. Usage is complimentary for paying overnight guests.

3.2 Airport Shuttle
- Schedule: A complimentary airport shuttle runs every hour on the hour from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- Booking: Guests must reserve their shuttle seat at the Concierge desk at least 4 hours prior to departure.

4. Pet Policy

4.1 Allowed Pets
- Species and Size: We welcome dogs and cats. The maximum weight limit is 50 lbs per pet.
- Limit: A maximum of two (2) pets are allowed per guest room.

4.2 Fees and Restrictions
- Pet Fee: A non-refundable pet fee of $75 per stay will be applied to your folio. Service animals are exempt from this fee.
- Unattended Pets: Pets may not be left unattended in guest rooms unless crated. If a pet is causing a noise disturbance, the guest will be contacted and must return to the property immediately.
- Restricted Areas: Pets are not permitted in the pool area, fitness center, spa, or indoor dining venues, with the exception of certified service animals.

5. Facility Rules and Restrictions

5.1 Smoking Policy
- 100% Smoke-Free: The Grand Horizon Resort & Spa is a 100% smoke-free facility. This includes cigarettes, cigars, e-cigarettes, and marijuana.
- Designated Areas: Smoking is only permitted in clearly marked designated outdoor smoking areas located 25 feet from all building entrances.
- Violation Fee: A $250 deep-cleaning fee will be charged to the guest's account if evidence of smoking is found in the room or on the room's balcony.

5.2 Quiet Hours
- Hours: Quiet hours are enforced between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM daily.
- Enforcement: Guests are expected to keep television volumes and voices at a respectable level. After one warning, guests violating quiet hours may be asked to leave the property without a refund.

6. Cancellation and Modification

6.1 Standard Cancellation
- Policy: For standard reservations, guests must cancel by 4:00 PM local time at least 48 hours prior to arrival to avoid a penalty of one night's room and tax.

6.2 Non-Refundable Rates
- Policy: Reservations booked under an "Advance Purchase" or "Non-Refundable" rate cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded under any circumstances.
